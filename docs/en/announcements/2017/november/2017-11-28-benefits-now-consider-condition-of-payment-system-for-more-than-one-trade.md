Benefits in the Promotions & Taxes module now recognize the payment system filter combined with the trade policy filter.

## What has changed?

Previously, benefits with payment system conditions were only applied to the main trade policy. As a result, benefits with payment system filters could not be applied to other trade policies.

In the current scenario, benefits now consider the payment system condition by trade policy. That means that a benefit with a payment system filter can be applied to more than one trade policy.

> ❗ It is important to note that this feature does not apply to third-party trade policies.

## Why did we make this change?

With this improvement, the retailer has more flexibility to configure his benefits.
