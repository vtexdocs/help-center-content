Black Friday 2026 is coming on **November 27**. Many stores start their campaigns before the official date, so now is the right time to prepare your operation for one of the most important retail events of the year.

![Black Friday 2026 preparation guide](https://cdn.statically.io/gh/vtexdocs/help-center-content/refs/heads/main/docs/en/announcements/2026/october/2026-10-06-black-friday-2026-preparation-guide-is-now-available.png)

As in previous years, we prepared a guide with recommendations and exclusive tips to help you get your store ready for the shopping season, including best practices for catalog, promotions, logistics, and store performance.

## What needs to be done?

Open the preparation guide and review the recommendations for your store. It is available to all VTEX clients and partners from the Admin homepage, or at:

```
https://{accountName}.myvtex.com/admin/shopping-season
```

Replace `{accountName}` with your store's account name.
