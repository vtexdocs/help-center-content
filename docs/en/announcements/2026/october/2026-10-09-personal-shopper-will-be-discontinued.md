The **Personal Shopper** app has been discontinued and is no longer available as of **September 30, 2026**. Assisted remote selling is now done on **VTEX CX Platform**, a generally available (GA) solution.

## What has changed?

The legacy Personal Shopper is no longer working. Stores that sell with assisted attendance should operate remote selling on VTEX CX Platform:

- An AI attendant automatically assists customers on WhatsApp.
- A human takes over the conversation when needed.
- The conversation can be transferred to the helpdesk, CRM, or contact center the team already uses, such as Zendesk and Salesforce.

This change evolves the legacy solution into AI attendants, keeping humans in the flow when the service requires intervention.

**Live Shopping** is not affected and remains available.

## What needs to be done?

To continue assisted remote selling, use [VTEX CX Platform](/en/docs/tutorials/vtex-cx-platform-category). If you have any questions, contact [VTEX Support](https://supporticket.vtex.com/support).
