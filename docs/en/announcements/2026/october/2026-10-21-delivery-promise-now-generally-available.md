[Delivery Promise](/en/docs/tutorials/delivery-promise) is now available to all merchants that use [Intelligent Search](/en/docs/tutorials/intelligent-search-overview). With this feature, while browsing, the customer sees only the products they can buy, based on available inventory and the [delivery options](/en/docs/tutorials/delivery-options) that are valid for their address.

Activation, which previously required a request to Support, is now done directly in the VTEX Admin, in a guided flow.

## What has changed?

With the general availability of Delivery Promise:

- **Activation in the VTEX Admin:** the new **Delivery Promise** page automatically checks whether your account is compatible and guides you through the activation steps.
- **Test before you publish:** you can activate test mode and configure the components in the store without affecting the production store.
- **Publishing to production from the Admin:** after you configure the store, you publish Delivery Promise for the customers of the selected trade policies.

The benefits for the shopping experience remain the same:

- Display of only the products that can be delivered to the customer's address.
- Filters by delivery and pickup options, shipping time frames (such as same-day or next-day delivery), and nearby pickup points.
- Greater visibility for the catalog of sellers that meet the customer's delivery criteria.
- Greater visibility for physical stores through pickup filters for nearby pickup points.

## What needs to be done?

To use Delivery Promise, your account must meet the [feature requirements](/en/docs/tutorials/delivery-promise#requirements), including the use of Intelligent Search. If the account is eligible, you can use Delivery Promise at no additional cost.

To activate the feature, follow the instructions in [Activating Delivery Promise](/en/docs/tutorials/activate-delivery-promise). Activation is done in a guided flow in the VTEX Admin, in **Storefront > Delivery Promise**, with the following steps:

1. **Activate test mode:** select the trade policies where Delivery Promise will be used and confirm the conditions of use. Test mode doesn't affect the production store.
2. **(Optional) Review delivery options:** choose which options appear as filters for customers in the store.
3. **Configure the storefront:** implement or configure the Delivery Promise components according to your store's technology.
4. **Publish to production:** publish the store version that contains the changes and activate Delivery Promise for customers.

>⚠️ The configuration and publishing steps require access to the store code. Only users with the account's Super Admin role can publish Delivery Promise to production.
