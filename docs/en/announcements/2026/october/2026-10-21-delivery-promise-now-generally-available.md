[Delivery Promise](/en/docs/tutorials/delivery-promise) is now available to all merchants using [Intelligent Search](/en/docs/tutorials/intelligent-search-overview). With this feature, buyers only see products they can purchase during browsing, based on available inventory and valid [delivery options](/en/docs/tutorials/delivery-options) for their address.

Previously, activation required a Support request. Now, you can activate the feature directly in the VTEX Admin through a guided process.

## What has changed?

With the general availability of Delivery Promise:

- **Activation via the VTEX Admin:** The new **Delivery Promise** page automatically checks if your account is compatible and guides you through the activation steps.
- **Test before publishing:** You can activate test mode and configure store components without affecting your live store.
- **Production publishing via the Admin:** After configuring your store, you publish Delivery Promise for shoppers of the selected sales channels.

The shopping experience benefits remain the same:

- Displaying only the products that can be delivered to the buyer's address.
- Filters by shipping and pickup options, delivery times (such as same-day or next-day delivery), and nearby pickup points.
- Greater visibility into the catalog of sellers that meet the buyer's delivery criteria.
- Increased visibility of physical stores to buyers through pickup filters at nearby pickup points.

## What needs to be done?

To use Delivery Promise, your account must fulfill the [feature requirements](/en/docs/tutorials/delivery-promise#requirements), including the use of Intelligent Search. If the account is eligible, you can use Delivery Promise at no additional cost.

To enable the feature, follow the instructions in the [Activating Delivery Promise](/en/docs/tutorials/activate-delivery-promise) guide. You can activate Delivery Promise through a guided process in the VTEX Admin under **Storefront > Delivery Promise**, following these steps:

1. **Activate test mode:** Select the sales channels where Delivery Promise will be used and confirm the Terms of Use. Test mode doesn't affect the production store.
2. **(Optional) Review delivery options:** Choose which options will be displayed as filters for shoppers in the store.
3. **Configure store:** Implement or configure the Delivery Promise components for your store technology.
4. **Publish to production:** Publish the store version with your changes and activate Delivery Promise for buyers.

> ⚠️ The configuration and publishing steps require access to your store's code. Only users with the Super Admin role in the account can publish Delivery Promise to production.
