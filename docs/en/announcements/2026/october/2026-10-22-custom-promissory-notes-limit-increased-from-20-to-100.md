Your store can now configure up to 100 custom notes payable. With this improvement, merchants who offer deferred payment through custom notes payable can provide a wider variety of payment conditions. The feature is available in open beta for all stores.

![Notes Payable section in the Custom Payments tab of the VTEX Admin](https://cdn.statically.io/gh/vtexdocs/help-center-content/refs/heads/main/docs/pt/announcements/2026/outubro/2026-10-22-promissorias-customizadas-limite-ampliado-de-20-para-100_1.png)

## What has changed?

Previously, you could configure up to 20 custom notes payable. Now, you can configure up to 100 in the **Notes Payable** section of the VTEX Admin, under **Store Settings > Payment > Settings > Custom Payments**.

The way to configure each note payable remains the same and is described in [Setting up payments with Notes Payable](https://help.vtex.com/en/docs/tutorials/setting-up-payments-with-notes-payable).

## Why did we make this change?

Many merchants, especially those operating in B2B, negotiate specific payment conditions with each customer, with different terms, billing rules, and margins. The previous limit of 20 notes payable restricted the number of conditions a merchant could offer.

## What needs to be done?

To access this improvement, open a ticket with [VTEX Support](https://supporticket.vtex.com/support) and request the activation of the new custom notes payable limit for your account.
