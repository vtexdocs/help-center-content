VTEX introduces the new [**Destination retail**](/en/docs/tutorials/destination-retail) page for publisher accounts. On this page, stores can declare that they want to receive traffic from [offsite campaigns](/en/docs/tutorials/vtex-ads-offsite-campaigns) and track which advertisers are directing traffic to their website.

## What has changed?

Publishers now have a space in **VTEX Ads** that brings together:

- Control of participation as a destination for offsite campaigns.
- Capture status on the surfaces (**Web** and **Mobile app**).
- List of advertisers with active campaigns directed to the store.

The decision to participate as a destination and the capture statuses on **Web** and **Mobile app** are independent. Enabling the **Available for new campaigns** toggle switch doesn't install, enable, or validate capture on any surface. Likewise, having active capture doesn't automatically declare that the store wants to participate as a destination.

## What needs to be done?

The page is already available to all **VTEX Ads** publishers. See the [Destination retail](/en/docs/tutorials/destination-retail) tutorial to learn the prerequisites, the eligibility conditions, and how to validate the capture statuses and view the advertisers sending traffic to your store.
