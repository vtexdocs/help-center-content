[**Bid Monitor**](https://help.vtex.com/en/docs/tutorials/bid-monitor-bid-competitiveness) is available in Open Beta in **VTEX Ads** for all accounts, in 1:1 campaigns. The indicator shows whether an ad's bid is competitive compared to competitors in the category and, when it isn't, suggests a more competitive amount.

## What has changed?

In the **VTEX Ads** Admin panel, an indicator appears next to the amount in the **CPC** or **CPM** column, both in the **Ad list**, which is the list in the **Ads** tab, and in the ads table of each campaign. The color and fill of the ring indicate one of four competitiveness levels: **Strong bid**, **Reasonable bid**, **Weak bid**, or **Very weak bid**.

The main characteristics of this feature are:

- **Suggested amount:** Appears below the bid amount. In the **Ad list**, the suggestion is read-only. To apply it, the advertiser clicks `View campaign` and, within the campaign, clicks the suggestion, checks the amount in the bid editor, and clicks `Apply`.
- **Access profiles:** The publisher sees the same ring and the same suggestion, without the `Apply` option.
- **Conditional display:** The ring doesn't appear in Network campaigns, in campaigns with an automatic objective-based strategy, in paused or draft ads, or when there are fewer than two competing campaigns on the same publisher, category, and ad type.

The indicator is a market reference, not a guarantee. The auction result also depends on the quality and relevance of the ad.

## Why did we make this change?

**Bid Monitor** lets the advertiser see whether the bid is competitive in 1:1 campaigns and apply a suggestion.

## What needs to be done?

No activation is needed. To view the indicator, log in to the **VTEX Ads** platform and click the **Ads** tab.
