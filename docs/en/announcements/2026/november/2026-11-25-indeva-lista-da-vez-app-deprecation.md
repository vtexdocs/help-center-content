The **Indeva – Lista da Vez** app has been deprecated. Lista da Vez is now available in VTEX Sales App, a more complete tool aligned with the VTEX ecosystem.

## What has changed?

Previously, sales associates used the **Indeva – Lista da Vez** app to organize the store's service queue. Now, [Lista da Vez](https://help.vtex.com/en/docs/tutorials/lista-da-vez) is a VTEX Sales App capability.

Team and metrics tracking continues to be done on the Indeva platform.

The **Indeva – Lista da Vez** app will no longer receive support after December 15, 2026.

## What needs to be done?

If your store still uses the **Indeva – Lista da Vez** app, migrate to VTEX Sales App by December 15, 2026:

1. [Open a support ticket](https://help.vtex.com/en/docs/tutorials/open-support-ticket-through-virtual-assistant-eva) to request VTEX Sales App access credentials for using Lista da Vez.
2. Install VTEX Sales App on the store device from [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.vtex.instore) or the [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/br/app/vtex-sales-app/id1100390867).
3. Log in to VTEX Sales App with the credentials sent by support.

To learn more, see [Lista da Vez (Beta)](https://help.vtex.com/en/docs/tutorials/lista-da-vez) and [Prerequisites for using VTEX Sales App](https://help.vtex.com/en/docs/tracks/prerequisites-for-using-vtex-sales-app).
