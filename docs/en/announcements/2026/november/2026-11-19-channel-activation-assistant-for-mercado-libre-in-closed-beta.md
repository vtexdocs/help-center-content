We're launching the closed beta of the **Channel Activation Assistant**, the VTEX assistant that guides merchants through Mercado Libre channel activation directly in the VTEX Admin, so you can start selling faster. In return, merchants who take part in the test phase must provide feedback to help evolve the product.

## Why did we build the assistant?

The assistant guides channel activation in the VTEX Admin. The following actions are possible:

- **Guided setup:** guides activation in five steps, an active Mercado Libre integration, sales channel, offer preferences (title and description), inventory strategy, and shipping strategy.
- **Clear order:** shows what is blocked and unlocks each step once the previous one is validated.
- **Category mapping:** maps your store categories to Mercado Libre categories and asks for confirmation before sending any data to the marketplace.
- **Real-time questions:** answers configuration questions based on official VTEX documentation.

## What needs to be done?

Access to the closed beta is limited. To request participation, contact the [support](https://supporticket.vtex.com/support) team.
