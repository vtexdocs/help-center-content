[Desk Copilot](https://help.vtex.com/en/docs/tutorials/desk-copilot-overview) is a new VTEX CX Platform feature that uses AI to analyze the ongoing conversation in [Live Desk](https://help.vtex.com/en/docs/tutorials/live-desk-overview) and suggest replies and actions for human representatives. Suggestions aren't executed automatically. The representative keeps control over the actions taken during the conversation.

This tool is now available to all VTEX CX Platform customers, in all regions.

## What has changed?

Previously, human representatives had to write each reply, search for products, assemble carts, check orders, and generate payment links manually. Now, Desk Copilot analyzes the conversation context and can suggest:

- Replies: ready-to-send messages, with a tone appropriate for the context.
- Product search: conversational search results displayed in carousels.
- Carts: cart suggestions with products.
- Order lookups: actions to check order information.
- Payment links: suggested links to help customers complete purchases.

The representative can copy the suggestion to the text field, edit it, or send it. **Desk Copilot** also uses transcribed audio and shared files, such as PDFs, as context for suggestions.

## Why did we make this change?

**Desk Copilot** was developed to make human support faster and more consistent, reducing manual tasks and making it easier to access commercial resources directly in **Live Desk**. The feature uses the same commercial agents available to AI agents and allows customization through custom agents, including the use of sales associate codes and UTM parameters in orders.

## What needs to be done?

No action is required. Desk Copilot is available to all VTEX CX Platform customers.

To learn more, see the [Desk Copilot](https://help.vtex.com/en/docs/tutorials/desk-copilot-overview) article.
