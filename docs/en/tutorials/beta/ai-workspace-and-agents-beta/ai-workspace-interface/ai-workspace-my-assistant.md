> ⚠️ AI Workspace is only available for selected accounts.

AI Workspace is the VTEX AI-powered work environment, where merchants track store performance, receive recommendations, and run their business together with AI agents. From a single screen, you can monitor key indicators, act on suggested initiatives, and chat with your assistant to perform day-to-day tasks.

![AI Workspace Home EN](https://cdn.statically.io/gh/vtexdocs/help-center-content/refs/heads/main/docs/en/tutorials/beta/ai-workspace-and-agents-beta/ai-workspace-interface/ai-workspace-my-assistant.png)

The following sections describe each part of the AI Workspace **My assistant** page.

## Performance indicators

This panel displays the store's main performance metrics, allowing you to quickly track business progress and get a complete overview of the operation. When you click the panel, the [Data Insights Agent](https://help.vtex.com/en/docs/tutorials/data-insights-agent) generates a performance report and opens a chat, where you can interact with the agent about the report. You can adjust the view by 7-, 30-, and 90-day time intervals.

The available metrics are:

- **Revenue:** Total revenue generated in the selected period.
- **Orders:** Number of orders placed.
- **Average order value:** Average value of the orders in the period.
- **Sessions:** Number of browsing sessions in the store.
- **Conversion rate:** Percentage of store visitors who make a purchase.
- **Items per order:** Average number of items per order in the period.

> ⚠️ In [headless](https://developers.vtex.com/docs/guides/headless-commerce) stores, **Sessions** and **Conversion rate** data isn't available.

## Recommended initiatives

This section highlights the initiatives proposed to improve store performance. Each initiative indicates the priority level, the impact area (such as GMV, conversion, or search), and the creation date, helping you quickly decide where to focus your efforts.

## Message box

At the bottom of the page, the message box is the central point of interaction with your AI assistant and has the following elements:

- **Text field:** Type a request or question for the assistant.
- **Add images and files:** Attach images and files to the chat context along with the text sent to the assistant.
- **Agent selection:** Select one of the installed AI agents to start a chat.
