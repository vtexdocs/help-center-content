> ⚠️ AI Workspace is only available for selected accounts.

The **Navigation menu** is a side panel on the left of the screen that groups the main areas of AI Workspace, helping you find what you need quickly and in an organized way.

![Navigation menu EN](https://cdn.statically.io/gh/vtexdocs/help-center-content/refs/heads/main/docs/en/tutorials/beta/ai-workspace-and-agents-beta/ai-workspace-interface/ai-workspace-navigation-menu.png)

At the top of the menu, you'll find the **Store title**, which identifies the account in use.

Right below it are the following navigation sections:

- **My assistant:** Access to the home page.
- **My initiatives:** View and manage recommended and ongoing initiatives.
- **Tasks:** View and manage tasks.
- **Chats:** Section with the list of chats with agents. Each item below is a button that opens the corresponding chat.

The following items are available at the bottom of the side menu:

- **🔍 Search:** Opens the search bar, allowing you to search across chats and initiatives.
- **🔔 Notifications:** Opens a component with the list of notifications.
- **Profile:** Circular icon with your profile picture. This component is also a button with the following options:
  - **My profile:** Opens the profile settings panel.
  - **Log out:** Logs out of your current AI Workspace session.
