> ⚠️ AI Workspace is only available for selected accounts.

**AI Workspace** is the VTEX AI-powered work environment. In it, people and AI agents collaborate to track store performance, identify opportunities, and carry out operational activities.

In a single environment, you can analyze indicators and recommendations, organize initiatives and tasks, delegate activities, and chat with specialized agents. When a decision requires human intervention, you can check the context, review the results, and define how the work should proceed.

## Agents

At VTEX, an **agent** is an entity based on large language models (LLMs), designed to perform specific commerce tasks in the VTEX ecosystem. It works as an interaction layer between users and platform services, turning goals expressed in natural language and proactively identified improvement points into concrete system actions.

Agents can run multistep workflows, recognize when an activity is complete, correct their actions when necessary, and stop or delegate the work in case of failure. To do so, they use tools that allow them to interact with VTEX services and external systems.

The main properties of an agent are:

- **Results-oriented:** Acts on clearly defined tasks and goals.
- **Triggering and interaction:** Can be triggered by natural language chats or by tasks, performing the corresponding operations in VTEX services.
- **Autonomy:** Runs multistep workflows, subject to human approval, and adjusts its actions according to the results obtained.
- **Tool use:** Selects the most suitable resources for each step of the workflow.
- **Controlled operation:** Follows the platform's behavior and security guidelines.

## How work is organized

AI Workspace structures the collaboration between people and agents around three main concepts:

- **Initiatives:** Represent problems found in the store, along with the diagnosis and suggested tasks to resolve the identified issue. An initiative can have one or more tasks, always within the same context.
- **Tasks:** Units of work with a narrow goal and scope. Each task has an assignee, who can be a person or an agent.
- **Chats:** Allow you to request information, delegate activities, track executions, review results, and approve actions. This structure combines the autonomous execution of agents with human oversight during the planning, execution, and validation of the work.
