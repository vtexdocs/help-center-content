If you want to terminate the contract and stop using the VTEX platform, you must [open a ticket with financial support](/en/docs/tutorials/opening-tickets-to-vtex-support#financial-and-commercial-support). To do so, follow the steps below:

> ⚠️ We recommend that you make a full backup of your data on the VTEX platform before deactivating the environment.

1. On the [VTEX Support](https://supporticket.vtex.com/support) page, click `Billing`.
2. In the **Subject of the request** field, select the `Contract termination` option.
3. Click `CONTINUE`.
4. In the **VTEX product to terminate** field, select the product whose contract you want to terminate, or **VTEX Platform** to stop using the entire platform.
5. In the **Reason for termination** field, select the reason for the termination.
6. Click `CONTINUE`.
7. In the **Describe in detail what happened** field, provide:

   - Whether you want to terminate the contract entirely or deactivate only some accounts.
   - The affected account or corporate name — or, in the case of partial deactivation, which accounts you want to deactivate.
   - The date on which you want to deactivate the accounts.

8. Click `SUBMIT`.

After the ticket is submitted, the process will start internally. Wait for the financial team's response.

> ⚠️ Depending on what's defined in your contract, the termination may be subject to a fine and compliance with a prior notice period. These terms also determine the date of your last billing.

## Documents for clients in Brazil

>⚠️ The instructions in this section apply only to clients billed in Brazil.

If your store operates in Brazil, include the following documents in the ticket:

- Store's CNPJ card.
- Articles of incorporation.
- Details of the legal representative who will sign the termination document: their RG, CPF, and email address.

Once the review is complete, the termination agreement will be sent for electronic signature via DocuSign by email.
