**[VTEX Ads](/en/docs/tracks/retail-media)** campaigns differ not only by ad type, such as **sponsored products**, **banners**, and **sponsored brands**, but also by who participates in them. The ad type defines the format shown to the user and is described in the [Retail Media](/en/docs/tracks/retail-media) article. The campaign model defines the relationship between the parties involved: who creates and funds the campaign, whose inventory displays the ad, who approves the creatives, who receives the traffic, and who tracks which metrics.

This article presents the three **VTEX Ads** campaign models (1:1 campaign, Network campaign, and offsite campaign), defines the roles specific to each model, and brings together, in a matrix, what each party does in each of them.

## Base roles

The advertiser, publisher, and marketing agency roles are common to all **VTEX Ads** campaigns and are described in [Retail media roles](/en/docs/tracks/retail-media-roles). In any campaign model, the marketing agency acts on behalf of the advertiser or the publisher and doesn't have a role of its own.

The Network and offsite models add roles that don't exist in the 1:1 campaign:

- **Network:** **VTEX Ads** publisher account that brings together several publishers. A campaign served on a network is a Network campaign.
- **Sub-publisher:** Publisher that is part of a network. In a Network campaign, the ad is displayed in the sub-publishers' stores.
- **Media publisher:** Party that displays an offsite campaign's ad outside the store that receives the traffic. Also called Publisher Offsite. In **VTEX Ads**, this side of the campaign appears to the client as **Offsite VTEX Ads**.
- **Destination retailer:** Store that receives the traffic from an offsite campaign and where the purchase happens. It's a **VTEX Ads** publisher account that agreed to participate in offsite campaigns on the **Destination retail** page. See [Destination retail](/en/docs/tutorials/destination-retail).

## 1:1 campaign

The 1:1 campaign brings together an advertiser and a publisher. The advertiser creates the campaign in their own account, funds the ad serving, and sets the destination URL of the ads. The publisher approves the creatives and displays the ads in their own inventory, such as the website, the app, or the physical store.

Because the ad is displayed in the publisher's store, that store receives the traffic and records the conversion. Each party tracks their side of the results: the advertiser sees the campaign's performance and cost, and the publisher sees the revenue generated by the ads displayed in their store. See [VTEX Ads metrics and attribution](/en/docs/tutorials/vtex-ads-metrics-and-attribution).

## Network campaign

The Network campaign brings together an advertiser, a network, and the sub-publishers that are part of it. The advertiser creates the campaign in their own account and funds the ad serving, as in the 1:1 campaign. The difference is on the publisher side: instead of a single inventory, the ad can be displayed in the stores of several sub-publishers.

By default, the Network campaign serves across the whole network. The network can enable the selection of specific sub-publishers. In that case, the advertiser selects the sub-publishers when creating the campaign, and the ad is displayed only in the selected stores.

The network approves the Network campaign's creatives. After approval, the ads are displayed in the stores of the sub-publishers included in the campaign, and each of those stores receives the traffic and records the conversions of the ads displayed in it.

## Offsite campaign

The offsite campaign brings together an advertiser, a media publisher, and a destination retailer. The ad is displayed outside the store that receives the traffic, on the media publisher's channel, and the click takes the user to the destination retailer, where the purchase happens.

In this model, the advertiser creates the campaign in the advertiser account, sets the media publisher and the destination retailer, and approves the campaign. This process is currently done with help from the **VTEX Ads** team.

Each party tracks a different portion of the results. The advertiser sees delivery and conversion metrics aggregated by campaign, without detail on which media publisher displayed the ad. The destination retailer sees which advertisers send traffic to the store and how many active campaigns each one has, without performance metrics. To understand the journey from the click to the attributed order and what each party needs to ensure, see [VTEX Ads offsite campaigns](/en/docs/tutorials/vtex-ads-offsite-campaigns).

## Role matrix by campaign model

The following table summarizes who is responsible for each stage in each campaign model:

| Responsibility | 1:1 campaign | Network campaign | Offsite campaign |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Create the campaign | Advertiser | Advertiser | **VTEX Ads** team, in the advertiser account |
| Fund the campaign | Advertiser | Advertiser | Advertiser |
| Define where the media is displayed | Publisher, in their own inventory | Network, across the whole network by default. When the network enables sub-publisher selection, the advertiser selects specific sub-publishers. | **VTEX Ads** team, when setting the campaign's media publisher |
| Approve creatives | Publisher | Network | **VTEX Ads** team |
| Define the destination URL | Advertiser | Advertiser | **VTEX Ads** team, when setting the campaign's destination retailer |
| Receive the traffic | Publisher's store | Store of the sub-publisher where the ad was displayed | Destination retailer |
| Record the conversion | Publisher's store | Store of the sub-publisher where the ad was displayed | Destination retailer, through the installed web script or mobile SDK |
| Track metrics | Advertiser and publisher, each with the view of their side | Advertiser and network, each with the view of their side | Advertiser, with metrics aggregated by campaign. Destination retailer, with the list of advertisers and active campaigns |
