Eligible stores can choose and subscribe to a standard [VTEX CX Platform](https://help.vtex.com/en/docs/tutorials/vtex-cx-platform-overview) plan from the VTEX Admin without a sales call or manual approval. Plans are based on monthly conversation volume.

## Prerequisites

Self-service subscription is available to stores that qualify for a standard conversation-volume plan.

The self-service flow is limited to standard plans and commercial terms. Contact the VTEX sales team if you need:

- Custom conversation volumes or negotiated pricing.
- Project or agent customizations.
- Marketing template dispatch pricing.

> ℹ️ The sales team also handles plan changes and upgrades for stores that already have a contract.

## Contacting a VTEX CX specialist

During your trial, you can contact a VTEX CX specialist from the CX Dashboard to ask about the platform, pricing, and contract options. You can schedule a call or send a message on WhatsApp. This option requires no setup and is only available during the trial.

> ℹ️ If your account is in Brazil, your specialist speaks Portuguese. For accounts outside Brazil, specialists speak Portuguese, Spanish, and English.

To contact your specialist, follow these steps:

1. In the VTEX Admin, go to **Storefront > VTEX CX Platform > CX Dashboard**.
2. Click `Talk to {Name}`, where `{Name}` is the name displayed in the contact widget.

    The specialist's contact options open.

3. Choose how to reach the specialist:
    - To schedule a call, click `Schedule a time` and select an available slot in the specialist's calendar. The calendar displays times in your local time zone.
    - To start a WhatsApp conversation, click `Send a message on WhatsApp`.

## Subscribing to a plan

To get started:

1. In the VTEX Admin, go to **Storefront > VTEX CX Platform**.
2. In the trial usage bar, click `View Plans`.
3. On **Billing Plans**, select a conversation volume.
4. Review the variable and excess costs, then select the checkbox to acknowledge the costs and accept the terms.
5. Confirm the subscription.

After you confirm, the plan is activated automatically and the project moves from trial to enterprise. Your acceptance of the terms formalizes the contract, so no further signature is needed. The platform records the acceptance, generates the contract documents and digital signature, provisions the account, and sets up billing with VTEX.

## Monitoring usage

Once the plan is active, the usage screen shows how much of the monthly conversation package your project has used. Conversations above the contracted volume are charged separately.

WhatsApp template costs aren't included in the conversation package. To learn how WhatsApp charges conversations, see [WhatsApp business API pricing](https://help.vtex.com/en/docs/tutorials/whatsapp-business-api-pricing).
