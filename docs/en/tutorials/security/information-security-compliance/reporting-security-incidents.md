This article explains how to identify and report security incidents related to VTEX services, including suspected system compromise, exposure of personal data, and incidents on the [VTEX CX Platform](/en/docs/tutorials/vtex-cx-platform-overview).

The reporting channel depends on the type of incident. Follow the recommended procedure to ensure a quick and effective response in case of suspected compromise or anomaly in your systems.

> If you suspect an incident, report it as soon as possible, even if you don't have all the information yet.

## What is a security incident?

A security incident is an event that can compromise the confidentiality, integrity, or availability of information, systems, or services.

Some examples are:

- Unauthorized access to systems or information.
- Exposure or leakage of data.
- Unauthorized alteration of information.
- System unavailability caused by attacks or suspicious activities.

> To learn how VTEX acts to prevent and handle these occurrences, see [Audit](/en/docs/tutorials/audit) and [Security incident response plan](/en/docs/tutorials/security-incident-response-plan).

## How to report a security incident

Identify the corresponding situation and use the indicated communication channel.

### General security incidents

To report suspected compromise, unauthorized access, or other suspicious activities in VTEX systems, go to [VTEX Support](/en/docs/tutorials/opening-tickets-to-vtex-support) and open a ticket with the following information:

- Detailed description of the incident.
- Date and time of the occurrence.
- Affected systems and possible impacts.
- Relevant evidence or logs, if available.

After opening the ticket, you'll receive a confirmation email with the ticket number. Updates and the continuity of the service will be handled by email.

### Incidents involving personal data

If you suspect unauthorized access, exposure, loss, or leakage of personal data, immediately report the occurrence to the VTEX Data Protection Officer (DPO) by email at [dpo@vtex.com](mailto:dpo@vtex.com).

> Personal data is information related to an identified or identifiable natural person, such as name, address, contact details, identification documents, banking data, and purchase history.

Include the following in your communication:

- Description of the incident.
- Categories of personal data potentially affected.
- Date and time of discovery or suspicion.
- Estimated number of people affected, if known.
- Possible cause or origin of the incident.
- Containment measures already adopted, if any.

> ⚠️ Incidents involving personal data may be subject to legal obligations to notify authorities and affected data subjects. In Brazil, ANPD Resolution CD/ANPD No. 15/2024 establishes, in applicable cases, a three-business-day deadline for the controller to report. For more information, see the [ANPD guidelines on reporting security incidents](https://www.gov.br/anpd/pt-br/canais_atendimento/agente-de-tratamento/comunicado-de-incidente-de-seguranca-cis).

### Incidents on the VTEX CX Platform

To report security incidents related to the [VTEX CX Platform](/en/docs/tutorials/vtex-cx-platform-overview), contact the specialized support team by email at [support.weni@vtex.com](mailto:support.weni@vtex.com).

Include the following in your communication:

- Description of the incident.
- Date and time of the occurrence.
- Possible impact on operations.
- Relevant evidence or logs, if available.

The specialized team will analyze the occurrence and respond according to the incident's priority.
