This article explains how to identify and report security incidents related to VTEX services, including suspected system compromises, exposure of personal data, and incidents involving [VTEX CX Platform](/en/docs/tutorials/vtex-cx-platform-overview).

The reporting channel depends on the type of incident. Follow the recommended procedure to ensure a quick and effective response if you suspect your systems have been compromised or detect unusual activity.

> If you suspect an incident, report it as soon as possible, even if you don't have all the information yet.

## What is a security incident?

A security incident is an event that can compromise the confidentiality, integrity, or availability of information, systems, or services.

Some examples are:

- Unauthorized access to systems or information.
- Data exposure or leakage.
- Unauthorized change of information.
- System unavailability caused by attacks or suspicious activity.

> To learn how VTEX prevents and responds to security incidents, see [Audit](/en/docs/tutorials/audit) and [Security incident response plan](/en/docs/tutorials/security-incident-response-plan).

## How to report a security incident

Identify the corresponding situation and use the indicated notification channel.

### General security incidents

To report a suspected system compromise, unauthorized access, or other suspicious activity in VTEX systems, go to [VTEX Support](/en/docs/tutorials/opening-tickets-to-vtex-support) and open a ticket with the following information:

- Detailed description of the incident.
- Date and time of the incident.
- Affected systems and potential impacts.
- Relevant evidence or logs, if available.

After opening a ticket, you'll receive a confirmation email with the ID number. Updates and support continuity will be handled via email.

### Incidents involving personal data

If you suspect unauthorized access, exposure, loss, or leakage of personal data, immediately report the incident to the VTEX Data Protection Officer (DPO) at [dpo@vtex.com](mailto:dpo@vtex.com).

> Personal data is information related to an identified or identifiable individual, such as name, address, contact details, identification documents, banking information, and purchase history.

Include the following in the notification:

- Incident description.
- Categories of personal data potentially affected.
- Date and time of the finding or suspicion.
- Estimated number of people affected, if known.
- Possible cause or origin of the incident.
- Containment measures taken, if any.

> ⚠️ Incidents involving personal data may be subject to legal obligations to notify authorities and affected individuals. The time limit for reporting an incident to authorities and affected parties depends on the regulations of the country where business operates.

### Incidents on VTEX CX Platform

To report security incidents related to [VTEX CX Platform](/en/docs/tutorials/vtex-cx-platform-overview), contact specialized support at [support.weni@vtex.com](mailto:support.weni@vtex.com).

Include the following in the notification:

- Incident description.
- Date and time of the incident.
- Potential operational impact.
- Relevant evidence or logs, if available.

The specialized team will analyze the incident and respond based on its corresponding priority.
