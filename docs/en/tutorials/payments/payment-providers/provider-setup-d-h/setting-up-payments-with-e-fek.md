At VTEX, it is possible to integrate with the E-fek payment provider. Through this provider, your store can make sales using Promissories.

To configure E-fek, follow the steps below:

1. In the VTEX Admin, go to **Store Settings > Payment > Providers**, or type **Providers** in the search bar at the top of the page.
2. On the providers screen, click the `New Provider` button.
3. Type the name **E-fek** in the search bar and click on the name of the provider.
4. In **Provider Authorization**, fill in the **App Key** and **App Token** fields with data provided by your E-fek account.
5. If you wish to modify the identification name to be displayed for the E-fek provider on the VTEX Admin screen, enter the information in the **Name** field in **Basic Information**.
6. Click `Save`.

To configure the payment methods to be processed by E-fek, access [Configuring Payment Conditions](/en/docs/tutorials/how-to-configure-payment-conditions).

To set special conditions on payment methods, go to [Configuring payment special conditions](/en/docs/tutorials/special-conditions).

After following the indicated steps, E-fek may take up to 10 minutes to appear at your store's checkout as a payment option.
