Discover the store information you can change in the VTEX Admin and when you need to open a ticket to request a change to your tax document.

## Changes via the VTEX Admin

You can update your store information in the VTEX Admin by accessing the **Billing** module. To do so, click your avatar, identified by your email initial, in the top right corner of the screen. Then, click **Billing**, and select the page you want to access.

You can change the following details:

- **Store name and address:** Edit this information on the **Billing > Registration information** page.
- **Contacts:** Add or edit store contacts on the **Billing > Billing contacts** page.

Learn more in [Billing information - Overview](/en/docs/tutorials/billing-module-overview).

## Updating the tax document

> ℹ️ The tax document legally and fiscally identifies the company and varies depending on the country of operation.

The store tax document can't be changed directly in the VTEX Admin. To request this change, [open a ticket with Financial Support](https://supporticket.vtex.com/support).

> ℹ️ If your store operates in Brazil, see the article [Changing the store's CNPJ](/en/docs/tutorials/changing-the-stores-cnpj).
