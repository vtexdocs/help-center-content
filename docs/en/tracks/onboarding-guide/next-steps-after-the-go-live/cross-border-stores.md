An international store, also called a cross-border store, is a type of [multistore](https://help.vtex.com/docs/tutorials/managing-a-multistore), which at VTEX means an account with multiple [bindings](/en/docs/tutorials/what-is-binding) and different sales channels for each one of its local stores.

Cross-border stores can reach buyers worldwide by offering localized shopping experiences.

If you want to reach customers worldwide and provide a localized shopping experience, [open a ticket](https://support.vtex.com/) expressing your interest in becoming a cross-border store.

A cross-border store helps you:

- Boost business growth by selling products in global markets.
- Build an international brand reputation.
- Offer local or trusted payment methods in each market.
- Manage all local stores in the same VTEX environment.

## Concepts

Before setting up a cross-border store on the VTEX platform, it's important to understand some concepts: [multistore](#multistore), [binding](#binding), and [sales channel](#sales-channel).

### Multistore

A multistore or multidomain is a second store managed within the same VTEX Admin. This feature is typically used when the store requires some specific environment (such as support for B2B sales) or when a store has multiple brands with similar logistics and payment methods.

> ℹ️ Learn more in [Managing a multistore](https://help.vtex.com/docs/tutorials/managing-a-multistore).

### Binding

Binding is the process of linking a website to one of the stores available in your account. Creating a binding for your account is one step in setting up a custom store website for a specific country or other target audience.

> ℹ️ Learn more in [What is binding?](/en/docs/tutorials/what-is-binding).

### Sales channels

A sales channel defines the content to be displayed on a website. Sales channels contain settings regarding catalog, prices, promotions, logistics, customer segmentation, and payments for different sales strategies.

> ℹ️ Learn more in [How sales channels work?](https://help.vtex.com/docs/tutorials/how-trade-policies-work)

## Multi-language stores

Since a cross-border store can have multiple bindings and reach buyers worldwide, internationalizing your website and catalog content is key to a personalized shopping experience.

To internationalize your store content, contact your developer team and see our documentation on [Internationalization](https://developers.vtex.com/docs/guides/vtex-io-multi-language-stores) and [Translating Catalog content](https://developers.vtex.com/docs/guides/catalog-internationalization).
