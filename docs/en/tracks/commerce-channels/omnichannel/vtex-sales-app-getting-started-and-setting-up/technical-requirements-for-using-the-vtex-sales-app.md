This article describes the technical requirements for using **VTEX Sales App**.

> ℹ️ Some specific features of **Sales App** may have additional technical requirements. This article covers basic usage of **VTEX Sales App**.

## Required equipment

The store must have a device to use **Sales App**, such as a desktop computer, tablet, smartphone, or touchscreen kiosk.

> ⚠️ We recommend setting aside a secure space in your store to keep your equipment.

### Tablets, smartphones, and touchscreen kiosks

Ideally, the device should be updated to the latest version of the operating system and, at a minimum, it must be compatible with:

- The three most recent versions of **iOS**
- The four most recent versions of **Android**

The device must have an internet connection via Wi-Fi or 4G/5G. Ideally, the mobile device should have a 4G/5G SIM card so the store keeps running normally even if the Wi-Fi network has issues.

> ℹ️ To keep your operation running smoothly, we recommend having more devices than sales associates. This way, a device can be quickly replaced if it fails, gets damaged, or runs out of battery.

### Computers

To use **Sales App** on a computer, the device must meet the following requirements:

- **Windows:**
  - **Windows 10** operating system or later.
  - **4 GB** RAM or more.

- **macOS:**
  - **macOS 13 Ventura** operating system or later.
  - **4 GB** RAM or more.

### Router and internet

An internet connection is essential for using **Sales App**. The minimum requirements and additional recommendations are:

| Equipment | Minimum requirements                                                                                                                      | Additional recommendations                                                                                                                                                 |
| :-------- | :---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Router    | 2.4 GHz standard (802.11n) and device-to-router connection of 20 Mbps. | 5 GHz standard (802.11ac) and device-to-router connection of 40 Mbps. The smartphone must be dual-band. |
| Internet  | 2 Mbps link speed on the device (result of a speed test on the mobile device).                         | 5 Mbps link speed on the device (result of a speed test on the mobile device).                                                          |

## Optional equipment

Although not required, we recommend the following equipment for the best experience with **Sales App**.

### Barcode scanner

**Sales App** has a native barcode reader that uses the smartphone camera to scan product barcodes. However, a dedicated device performs better for this function.

To speed up purchases, we recommend having a barcode scanner available, especially when using a desktop.

> ℹ️ To use the barcode scanner, add the product barcode in the Catalog, as described in [Register the barcode of the SKUs for VTEX Sales App](https://help.vtex.com/docs/tracks/register-the-barcode-of-the-skus-for-vtex-sales-app).

### Payment terminal

Credit and debit card payment terminals with Bluetooth or USB technology allow physical stores to process payments. They're offered by payment partners, whether acquirers or sub-acquirers.
