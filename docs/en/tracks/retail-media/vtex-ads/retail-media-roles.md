In a retail media operation, three main roles stand out: **advertiser**, **publisher**, and **marketing agency**. See what each role means and how they work in the context of VTEX Ads:

## Main roles

| Role | Explanation |
| ---- | ---- |
| **Advertiser** | Brand or manufacturer that wants to promote its products in retailers' sales channels. In onsite campaigns in **VTEX Ads**, the advertiser sets up and manages campaigns in sponsored shelves, banners, and other placements on the publisher's website. |
| **Publisher** | Retailer that offers advertising spaces for monetization. In onsite campaigns in **VTEX Ads**, the publisher controls the available spaces, approves campaigns, and tracks the results of the ads displayed in their store. |
| **Marketing agency** | Company or outsourced team that acts on behalf of the advertiser or the publisher. In the context of VTEX Ads, the agency can plan, set up, track, and optimize campaigns, ensuring the best use of media and alignment with the client's goals. Agencies are especially common when the advertiser or the publisher doesn't have an in-house team dedicated to media operations. |

## Roles in Network and offsite campaigns

The Network and offsite models add roles that don't exist in the 1:1 campaign:

| Role | Explanation |
| ---- | ---- |
| [**Network**](/en/docs/tutorials/vtex-ads-campaign-models-and-roles) | Publisher account that brings together several publishers to serve Network campaigns. |
| [**Sub-publisher**](/en/docs/tutorials/vtex-ads-campaign-models-and-roles) | Publisher that is part of a network and can display ads from Network campaigns. |
| [**Destination retail**](/en/docs/tutorials/vtex-ads-campaign-models-and-roles) | Store that receives the traffic from an offsite campaign and where the purchase happens. |
