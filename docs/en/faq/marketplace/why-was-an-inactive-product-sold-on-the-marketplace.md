This can happen because [deactivating an SKU in VTEX does not deactivate it on the marketplace](/en/docs/faq/why-isnt-my-product-deactivated-on-the-marketplace). That step is not part of the integration, so the listing can stay active on the partner.

To deactivate the SKU on the marketplace, contact the marketplace and follow their process.

If a customer already bought the item, you can activate it in VTEX so the order integrates, or refuse the integration.
